# Paw OS

Paw OS is PocketPaw's agent workspace. You chat with AI agents, and they build interactive tools, documents and sites for you. Your work lives in workspaces: chats, pockets (small interactive apps and dashboards), notes, files, agents with their own instructions and memory, and sites you can publish to the web. You can connect services you already use, such as email, calendar, storage or code hosting, so your agents can work with them when you ask.

Paw OS runs in the browser at [os.pocketpaw.xyz](https://os.pocketpaw.xyz) and as a desktop app. It is in early access and free to use for now: new accounts get early-access credits for AI features.

Paw OS is made by Qbtrix Innovations, the company behind PocketPaw.

## Signing in with Google

Paw OS uses Google Sign-In only to create your account and sign you in. It requests your name, email address and profile picture (the openid, email and profile scopes), and nothing else. Signing in with Google does not give Paw OS access to your Gmail, Google Drive or any other Google data.

How we handle this information is described in the [Paw OS Privacy Policy](/privacy), including how we use Google user data (section 2).
