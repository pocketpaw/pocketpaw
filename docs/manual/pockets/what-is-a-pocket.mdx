# What is a Pocket

**What it is.** A Pocket is a workspace you build for one job — a dashboard, tracker, or tool made of widgets. Each Pocket holds its own data, its own widgets, and its own chat.

**When you'd use it.** Use a Pocket when you want a focused screen for a single area of work, like revenue, hiring, or a project, instead of scattering it across other tools.

## Open the Pockets area
1. Go to **/pockets** in Paw OS by PocketPaw.
2. You land on the grid view. Each card is one Pocket.
3. A card shows the Pocket name, a short description, the widget count, and when it was last active.
4. Click any card to open that Pocket.

## Find a Pocket
1. Use the left sidebar to filter by category — for example **All Pockets**, **Deep Work**, **Research**, **Data & Metrics**, **Business**, or **Custom**.
2. The grid updates to show only Pockets in that category.

## What's inside a Pocket
Open a Pocket and you'll see a toolbar of views across the top:
- **Widgets** — the main canvas where your charts, lists, and cards live.
- **Data** — the data sources and records behind the Pocket.
- **Files** — files attached to this Pocket.
- **Automations** — scheduled or triggered actions.
- **Actions** — items waiting for your approval.
- **Audit** — a log of what happened in the Pocket.

## Tips
- The chat panel on the right is tied to the open Pocket. Ask it to change the Pocket and it edits what you're looking at.
- A small live dot on a card means the Pocket is actively updating.

## If something's off
- If you see "Cloud session expired — changes are saved locally only," click **Re-login**. Until you do, your edits stay on this device only.
- An empty grid with "No pockets yet" just means you haven't made one. See [Create a Pocket](/manual/pockets/create-a-pocket).
