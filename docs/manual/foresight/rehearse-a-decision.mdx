# Rehearse a Decision

**What it is.** Foresight runs a decision through a group of AI personas before you make it. You see how each one would react, where they would push back, and what you might miss. The product calls a saved decision a **Rehearsal**.

**When you'd use it.** Before a renewal, a price change, or a policy rollout — when you want to practice the call and read the reactions first.

## Start from a template
1. Go to **/foresight**. The page is titled **Rehearsals**.
2. Click **New rehearsal** (top right). A window opens titled **Rehearse the future.**
3. Pick one of the three cards: **A single decision**, **A market response**, or **An org rollout**. Each card shows an example, an estimated time, and how many personas it uses.
4. Click **Start** on the card you want.
5. The editor opens, pre-filled from that template. Adjust the wording for your situation.
6. Click **Create rehearsal** to save it.

If the page has no rehearsals yet, you'll see **Nothing rehearsed yet. Pick a template to start.** with the same three cards shown inline — pick one to begin without opening the window.

## Describe your own decision instead
1. On **/foresight**, click **New rehearsal**, or click **Rehearse a decision** in the toolbar at the top.
2. In the window, scroll to **Or describe your own decision**.
3. Type your decision in plain language, for example: "Rehearse our Q4 renewal across 5 tenant personas."
4. Press **Cmd+Enter** (or **Ctrl+Enter**), or click the send button.
5. The assistant sidebar opens and takes the decision from there.

## Run a rehearsal and read the result
1. On **/foresight**, find the rehearsal in the list.
2. Click **Run** (or **Run again** if it has run before).
3. Wait for the run to finish — most take 30 to 60 seconds.
4. When it's done, the rehearsal's results open. The progress badge at the top of the page walks the four steps: **PICK → RUN → RESULTS → LEARN**.
5. Once a rehearsal has a finished run, its button changes to **View** — click it to reopen the latest result.

## Tips
- Open a rehearsal card's arrow (chevron) to see its **Run history** and a small colored **Trend** strip — green for a clean run, red for a failed one.
- Use **Branch** on a card to spin off a variant of a decision without losing the original.
- The **Refresh** button (top right, circular arrow) reloads the list if a run you started elsewhere isn't showing yet.

## If something's off
- Test or scratch rehearsals are hidden by default. A footer link reads **N hidden test rehearsals — show all** — click it to reveal them. Nothing is deleted, only hidden.
- A card that says **Draft · never run** simply hasn't been run yet. Click **Run** to start it.
