# Rooms, direct messages, groups, and channels

**What it is.** The sidebar on the chat screen groups all your conversations into four sections: **ROOMS**, **DIRECT MESSAGES**, **GROUPS**, and **CHANNELS**. Each one is a different way to organize who you're talking to.

**When you'd use it.** To find a conversation, start a new one, or set up a shared space for a topic or a team.

## Find your way around the sidebar
1. **ROOMS** — focused workspaces. **General** at the top is your default agent conversation; the rest are scoped to a specific workspace area.
2. **DIRECT MESSAGES** — one-to-one chats with a teammate or an agent.
3. **GROUPS** — a private conversation with a handful of named people.
4. **CHANNELS** — open or private spaces organized by topic, marked with **#**.

Click any section header to collapse or expand it. Use the **Search rooms…** box at the top to jump to any conversation by name.

## Start a direct message
1. In the sidebar, find the **DIRECT MESSAGES** section.
2. Click the **+** next to it (it says **New DM**), or use the **New message** button at the top.
3. In the **New chat** window, search by name, agent, or email.
4. Click a teammate or an agent to open the chat.

## Create a group
1. Find the **GROUPS** section and click the **+** (it says **New Group**).
2. Type a name in the **Group name…** box.
3. Click **Create**.
4. Open the group and use **Manage members** in the header to add people.

## Join or create a channel
1. Find the **CHANNELS** section.
2. Click the search icon (**Browse channels**) to see channels you can join, then click one to join it.
3. To make a new one, click the **+** (it says **New Channel**) and fill in the form.
4. A **#** marks open channels; a lock marks private ones.

## Tips
- A blue dot or a number badge next to a conversation means unread messages.
- The online dot next to a direct-message avatar shows whether that person is online.
- You can rename a channel and open its settings from the row's hover controls.

## If something's off
- Don't see a channel? It may be private. Ask the channel owner to add you, or check **Browse channels** for ones you can join.
- A long list of rooms scrolls inside its own section, so the other sections stay reachable below it.
- Deleting a direct message removes the conversation from your sidebar — use the trash icon on the row, and confirm before it's gone.
