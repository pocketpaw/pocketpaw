# Meetings

**What it is.** The Meetings screen lists your upcoming and past video meetings in one timeline. You can schedule a Zoom or Google Meet, join a live call, and open transcripts and recordings.

**When you'd use it.** To set up a call, jump into one that's running, or look back at notes from a meeting that already happened.

## See your meetings
1. Open **Meetings** from the left.
2. The timeline shows your meetings with the closest one highlighted at the top.
3. Use the filter row — **All**, **External**, **Group**, **Calendar** — to narrow what's shown.
4. Click the refresh icon if you've just made changes elsewhere and want the latest.

## Schedule a meeting
1. Click **New meeting** (or **Schedule a meeting** when the list is empty).
2. In the **Schedule a meeting** window, pick the date and time.
3. Choose a length from the dropdown — 15 min up to 2 hours.
4. Add a short note in the **What's this meeting about?** box.
5. Save to put it on the timeline.

You can also just ask your agent in chat to set up a meeting for you, instead of using this form.

## Join a meeting
1. Find the meeting in the timeline.
2. When it's live, click **Join** to enter the call.
3. In the call, use the controls to **Mute**/**Unmute**, turn your camera on or off, **Share screen**, and **Leave**.

## Open a transcript or recording
1. Click a past meeting to open its details.
2. If a transcript is ready, it loads in the panel. A mark on the row shows when a transcript is available.
3. A still-processing transcript shows a short notice — check back in a bit.

## Tips
- A meeting marked **Live** is happening now; **Transcript ready** means notes are available.
- The provider tag shows whether a meeting is **Zoom** or **Google Meet**.
- Send a recording bot to a meeting to get a transcript automatically.

## If something's off
- The list is empty until you schedule something or connect a calendar. Connect Google Calendar to auto-create meetings from your invites.
- Transcripts depend on a recording bot having joined the call. No bot, no transcript.
- This screen is wired to scheduling, joining, and transcripts, but calendar-connect setup lives in **Settings**. Confirm your provider is connected there if meetings aren't syncing.
