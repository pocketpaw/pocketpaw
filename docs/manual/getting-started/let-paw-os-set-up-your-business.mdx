# Let Paw OS by PocketPaw set up your business

**What it is.** A short, guided setup that stands up your workspace for you. You answer a few prompts and Paw OS creates your workspace, sets your preferences, and can install a ready-made starter kit so you're working in minutes.

**When you'd use it.** Right after you sign in for the first time as the owner of a new workspace.

## Walk through setup
1. **Tell it your name.** The first screen asks "What should I call you?" Type your name and click **That's me!**
2. **Pick an avatar.** Choose an emoji to represent you.
3. **Pick your AI brain.** Choose how to power your assistant:
   - **Free & Local** — runs on your own machine, no account needed.
   - **Powerful** — uses a smarter model and needs an API key.
   - Or click **Skip for now — I'll add an AI later**.
4. **Name your workspace.** Type a workspace name (for example, your company name). A URL slug is suggested for you, and Paw OS checks in real time whether it's available. Click **Create workspace**. If you already have a workspace, you can choose **Continue with `<name>`** instead.
5. **Choose a theme** and confirm a few **preferences** (notifications and sound). These save automatically.
6. **Choose how to start:**
   - **Sales OS** (marked Recommended) — installs a sales assistant, a pipeline, and starter widgets in seconds.
   - **Browse templates** — start from one of the pre-built templates.
   - **Skip — I'll set up later**.
7. **Invite your team** (optional) — add teammates now or do it later.
8. **You're ready.** A summary screen confirms your name, workspace, and what got installed, then drops you into your workspace.

## Tips
- Nothing here is permanent. The setup screen says so directly: "You can always change this later."
- You can skip the AI step, the starter step, and the invite step and still finish setup.
- The URL slug must be lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens. A green check means it's available.
- **Sales OS** is the fastest way to see Paw OS doing real work — it stands up a working assistant and pipeline for you.

## If something's off
- **"That slug is already taken."** Tweak the workspace name or edit the slug until you see the green "is available" check.
- **"Failed to install Sales OS."** Try again, or choose **Browse templates** or **Skip** and set up later from inside the workspace.
- **It skipped setup and dropped you straight into the app.** If you've set up before on another device, Paw OS recognizes you and skips the wizard rather than making you redo it.
- **Note on scope.** In this build, setup creates your workspace and installs a starter kit; it does not auto-generate a full custom business configuration from a single description. The "auto-setup" experience is the guided wizard plus the Sales OS / template install.
