# Create your account

**What it is.** Paw OS by PocketPaw signs you in with a Paw Cloud account. Your account is what lets you create a workspace, invite your team, and keep your assistant and data in sync across devices.

**When you'd use it.** The first time you open Paw OS, before you set anything up.

## Sign in to get started
1. Open Paw OS. You land on a screen that says **Sign in to set up your workspace**.
2. Click **Sign in to Paw Cloud**.
3. In the sign-in panel, type your **Email** and **Password**.
4. Click **Connect**.
5. If your account uses a one-time code, type the **Authenticator code** when asked. The app verifies it and signs you in.
6. Wait for **Workspace ready**, then you move straight into setup.

## Tips
- If a code is rejected, you can choose **Use a backup code** instead of the authenticator code.
- Caps Lock shows a warning under the password field if it's on.

## If something's off
- **"Wrong account."** If you opened an invite link while signed in as a different email, Paw OS tells you the invite was sent to another address. Use the **Sign out and use `<email>`** button, then open the link again.
- **"Couldn't load your workspace."** Click **Try again**. If it keeps failing, **Sign out** and sign back in.
- **New to Paw OS?** Switch the sign-in panel to the **Create account** tab and enter your email, name, and a password. If a teammate sent you an invite, accepting it also creates your account (see "Join a workspace").
