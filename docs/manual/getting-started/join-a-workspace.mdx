# Join a workspace

**What it is.** When someone adds you to their team, they send an invite link. Opening that link gets you into their workspace, with the role they picked for you.

**When you'd use it.** You got an invite link by email and want to join your team's workspace.

## Join with an invite link
1. Open the invite link from your email. Paw OS by PocketPaw shows **Validating invite…**, then a card that says **Join `<workspace name>`**.
2. Check the role on the card — it reads "You've been invited as `<role>`." If a group was set, it also shows the group you'll join.
3. **If you don't have an account yet**, fill in the short form:
   - Your email is filled in for you and can't be changed.
   - Type your **Full Name**.
   - Set a **Password**. A checklist shows the requirements as you type.
   - Repeat it in **Confirm Password**.
   - Click **Create Account & Join**.
4. **If you already have an account**, the card shows you're signed in and offers **Accept & Join `<workspace name>`**. Click it.
5. You're welcomed by name and walked through a short setup: a welcome screen, an optional step to connect your own Gmail and calendar, picking an avatar, and a ready screen.

## Tips
- Connecting your **Gmail** and **Google Calendar** is optional and private to you — only your own assistant uses it, never your teammates or admins. You can skip it and connect later from Settings.
- Each step has a **Skip for now** option. Skipping doesn't break anything.
- You inherit the workspace's AI setup automatically, so you don't pick an AI provider when you join.

## If something's off
- **"This invite has expired."** Invites expire after 7 days. Ask whoever invited you to send a fresh one.
- **"Invite revoked"** or **"Invite already accepted."** The link is no longer usable. Ask your admin to resend.
- **"Wrong account."** The invite was sent to a different email than the one you're signed in with. Use **Sign out and use `<email>`**, then open the link again.
- **"Your account was created … but joining the workspace didn't go through."** Your account is fine and you're signed in — refresh the page to finish joining.
