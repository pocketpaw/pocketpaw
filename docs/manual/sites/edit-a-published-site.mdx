# Edit a Published Site

**What it is.** Each site has a builder screen where you preview it, change it by chatting, read the leads it captures, and manage its settings. You change a site by describing the edit, then publishing again so visitors see it.

**When you'd use it.** Your site is up and you want to tweak the copy, swap a section, add a custom domain, or check who filled out your form.

## Open a site's builder
1. Open **Sites** from the navigation.
2. Click the card for the site you want.
3. The builder opens. The bar at the top shows the site name, a status pill (**● Live**, **◐ Publishing…**, or **○ Draft**), and an **Open** button when the site is live.

## Change the site by chatting
1. In the builder, use the chat rail on the right. It is titled **Refine** with the prompt "Refine this site…".
2. Describe the change in plain words. For example: "Make the headline shorter and add a phone number to the footer."
3. Send the message and watch the **Preview** tab update.
4. When you're happy, click **Publish** in the top bar to push the change live.

## Check your leads
1. In the builder, click the **Leads** tab.
2. You'll see a table of everything submitted through your forms, with the time and form type.
3. If it says "No leads captured yet," nobody has submitted the form so far.

## Connect a custom domain
1. In the builder, click the **Settings** tab, then **Domains**.
2. Below the status list, find **Add a custom domain**.
3. Type your domain (for example, `www.yourbusiness.com`) and click **Add domain**.
4. Copy the single record shown and paste it at your domain registrar. Use the copy button next to it.
5. The status moves on its own: **Pending DNS → Verifying → ● Live**. You do not need to log in to Cloudflare.

## Tips
- The Preview always shows your latest draft, including changes you haven't published yet. Visitors only see what you've published.
- After you publish, the status pill may read **Publishing…** for a moment while the deploy finishes, then turn to **Live** on its own.
- The **Open** button only appears once the site is truly live. It opens the public page in a new tab.
- Use **Redeploy** under **Settings → Deploy** to push the current version out again if a deploy didn't take.

## If something's off
- A change you made in the Refine rail won't reach visitors until you click **Publish**. Refining alone updates the draft, not the live page.
- If you reach a "Site not found" message, the site may have been removed. Click **Back to sites** to pick another.
