# Publish a Site

**What it is.** Sites turns your work into a real website that anyone can visit. You start on the **Sites** screen, describe the page you want, and Paw OS by PocketPaw designs it and puts it online.

**When you'd use it.** You need a landing page, a portfolio, or a simple marketing site and you don't want to touch code or a website builder.

## Describe a site and put it online
1. Open **Sites** from the navigation.
2. Look at the chat rail on the right. It says "Describe a site and I'll design + publish it."
3. Type what you want in plain words. For example: "A dentist landing page with an appointment booking form."
4. Press send. A building card appears in the grid and walks through three steps: **Designing the page → Publishing → Live**.
5. When the site is ready, the screen opens its builder so you can preview it and share the link.

## Start from a suggestion
1. On the Sites screen, the chat rail shows starter chips: **Dentist landing page**, **Bakery page**, **SaaS waitlist**, **Personal portfolio**.
2. Click a chip. It fills the message box with a full description.
3. Edit the wording if you like, then press send.

## Publish a page you already built
1. On the Sites screen, click **Publish a pocket** at the top.
2. A picker opens listing the workspace items you can publish.
3. Find the one you want and click **Publish** on its row.
4. When it finishes, the screen opens the new site's builder.

## Tips
- Be specific. Say what sections you want (hero, services, contact form) and the business it's for. More detail gives a better first draft.
- The **All / Live / Draft** buttons at the top filter your sites. **Live** means it is actually serving to visitors; **Draft** covers both new sites and ones still publishing.
- The **Custom** button at the top is on by default. Leave it on for hand-crafted pages. Turn it off to use the standard template instead.

## If something's off
- If the building card sits on a step for a long time, use the refresh icon at the top right to reload your sites, then check whether the new site appeared in the grid.
- "Publishing…" is not the same as "Live." A site can be published but still finishing its deploy. Wait for the status to turn to **Live** before sharing the link.
- The **Publish a pocket** picker only lists existing items. If it's empty, build a page first, then come back and publish it.
