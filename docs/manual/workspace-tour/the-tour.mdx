# Your workspace

**What it is.** Your workspace is the main screen of Paw OS by PocketPaw. A rail across the top switches between areas, the center shows your pinned widgets and a greeting, and a chat sits in the corner so you can ask for things at any time.

**When you'd use it.** Every day. This is the screen you land on when you open Paw OS.

## Move between areas with the top rail
The rail at the top of the window has five primary tabs:

1. **Foresight** — rehearse a decision before you commit to it.
2. **Pockets** — your saved apps and dashboards.
3. **Files** — artifacts your agents produce, plus your own uploads.
4. **Chat** — talk to Paw OS in a full conversation.
5. **Agents** — build, tune, and manage your AI crew.

Everything else (Deep Work, Calendar, Meetings, Activity, Knowledge, Sites, Belt, Studio, Settings) sits under **More**. Click a tab to switch. The active tab is highlighted. Your home screen, with pinned widgets and the corner chat, is the **Home** entry at the far left.

## Read your home screen
1. The top-left corner shows the time and a greeting with your name.
2. Below the greeting, a small line shows quick counts (agents, pockets, and actions today).
3. The **Home:** pill lets you pick which pocket frames your home. Click it, then choose a pocket from the list (or **No home pocket**).

## Pin and arrange widgets
1. Your widgets live under the **Pinned** heading in the center of the screen.
2. To add one, click **+ Add widget** and choose what to pin.
3. To remove one, use the unpin control on the widget.
4. If a widget shows live data, use **Refresh** in the Pinned header to pull the latest. (Refresh only appears when a widget is set up to read live data.)

## Ask the corner chat
1. The chat sits in a corner of the home screen as a small pill.
2. Click it to expand, type a request, and press Enter.
3. You can also ask it to add a widget to your home — for example, "add my calendar to my home" — and it will pin one for you.
4. Use the panel toggle in the chat to dock it as a right-hand sidebar instead of a floating pill.

## Tips
- The greeting changes with the time of day, so a "Good evening" instead of "Good morning" is expected, not a bug.
- "Files" in the top rail is your home screen. The full file browser is a separate area covered in its own page.
- Picking a home pocket changes the context the corner chat works in, so set it to the pocket you care about most.

## If something's off
- A blank center with an add prompt usually means you haven't pinned anything yet — that's normal for a fresh workspace.
- If a widget never loads data, check that its data source is connected. An admin may need to wire it up.
- The **Refresh** button is hidden when nothing on the home screen reads live data — that's expected, not missing.
