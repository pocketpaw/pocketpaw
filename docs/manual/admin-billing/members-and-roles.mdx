# Members and roles

**What it is.** The Members page lists everyone in your workspace and the invites you've sent but that nobody has accepted yet. You add people, set what each person can do, and remove people from here.

**When you'd use it.** Use it when you bring someone onto the team, change what they're allowed to do, or take away their access.

## Add someone to the workspace
1. Open Settings, then under Workspace pick **Members**.
2. Click **Invite** (top right). You only see this button if you're an owner or admin.
3. Enter the person's email and pick their role.
4. Send the invite. It moves to the **Pending invites** tab until they accept.

## Change someone's role
1. On the **Members** tab, find the person.
2. In the Role column, open the dropdown and pick the new role.
3. The change saves on its own. A small confirmation appears.

## Remove someone
1. Find the person on the **Members** tab.
2. Click the trash icon at the end of their row.
3. Type their name (or email) exactly to confirm, then click **Remove**. They lose access right away.

## What each role can do
- **Owner** — full control, including deleting the workspace.
- **Admin** — manages members and settings, but can't make someone an owner.
- **Member** — uses the workspace, with no admin powers.

(Click the small info icon next to the Role heading to see this same explanation in the app.)

## Tips
- Pending invites live on their own tab. The number badge shows how many are still waiting.
- You can re-send an invite that's still pending, but there's a short cooldown between sends.
- Members (the basic role) see the list as read-only — no menus or buttons to change anyone.

## If something's off
- You can't change or remove yourself from this page — that's on purpose.
- An admin can't promote anyone to **Owner**, and can't touch an existing owner. Only an owner can do that.
- If you try to remove or demote the last owner, the workspace blocks it and shows a message. Promote someone else to owner first.
