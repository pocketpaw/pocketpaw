# Plans and billing

**What it is.** Where you'd manage your subscription, see your current plan, and update payment details.

**When you'd use it.** When you sign up, change tiers, or update a card.

## Heads up — not in the app yet
There's no billing, plans, or subscription page in the current build. The settings menu has Profile, Preferences, Notifications, Account & sessions, API keys, and a Workspace section — but nothing for payment or plans. This page is a placeholder so the manual has a home for billing once it ships.

If you need anything about your plan or invoices today, contact your account contact directly rather than looking for it in the app.

## Tips
- This page will be rewritten the moment a real billing surface exists. Until then, treat it as a known gap, not a feature.

## If something's off
- You won't find a billing entry in Settings — that's expected for now, not a bug.
- A human writer should confirm whether billing is handled outside the app (sales, contract, or a separate portal) and update this page accordingly.
