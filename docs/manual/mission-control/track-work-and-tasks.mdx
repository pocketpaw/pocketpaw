# Track work and tasks

**What it is.** Deep Work is the single screen where every task in your workspace lands — work your agents are doing, work waiting on you, and work that's already shipped. You watch it, approve it, reassign it, and create new tasks from here.

**When you'd use it.** Open it when you want one place to see what's in flight and to act on anything that needs your decision.

## Open Deep Work
1. Open the navigation rail and select **Deep Work** (the compass icon).
2. The toolbar shows your scope as a trail: workspace, then project, then pocket.
3. The **Feed** tab is the default view.

## Find the task you care about
1. Stay on the **Feed** tab. Tasks are grouped into sections:
   - **The Tray** — needs your eyes.
   - **Agents in flight** — work your agents are doing on their own.
   - **Delegated** — work assigned to another person.
   - **Pawprints** — shipped today.
   - **Snags** — failed, reverted, or blocked.
2. Use the filter pills at the top of the feed (**All**, **Tray**, **In flight**, **Delegated**, **Pawprints**, **Snags**) to jump to one section. The number on each pill is the count.
3. Type in the **Filter by title…** box in the toolbar to narrow by name. Press `⌘K` to jump straight to it.
4. In the left rail, click a **Pocket** or an **Agent** to scope the feed to just that one. Click it again to clear.

## Open and act on one task
1. Click any row to open the detail panel on the right.
2. The panel shows the task summary, who it's assigned to, its pocket, and a full activity timeline.
3. When a task is waiting for your decision, use the buttons at the bottom:
   - **Approve** — accept the work.
   - **Reject** — turn it back.
   - **Reassign** — hand it to a different person.
   - **Snooze 24h** — push it out a day.
4. Add a note in the **Add a comment or note…** box and press Enter.
5. Press `Esc` to close the panel.

## Approve several tasks at once
1. Hover a row and a checkbox appears on the left. Click it to select.
2. Hold Shift and click another row to select a range.
3. A bar appears at the top with **Approve**, **Reject**, and **Snooze 24h**. Pick one to apply it to everything you selected.
4. Click **(clear)** to drop the selection.

## Create a new task
1. Click **new** in the feed header, or press `⌘N`.
2. Type what needs doing in the title field.
3. Set who does it: open the assignee picker and choose a person under **Humans** or an agent under **Agents**.
4. Set **Priority**, and optionally a **Pocket**, **Project**, **Cycle**, and a due date.
5. Click **Create**. Turn on **Create more** first if you want to add several in a row.

## Tips
- The strip above the feed counts your outcomes — **shipped**, **pending**, **reverted**, **failed**. Switch the window with **All**, **1h**, **24h**, **7d**.
- Assigning a task to an agent delegates it; assigning to a person keeps it as human work.
- A rose **Blocked by** badge means an upstream task has to finish first. You can't approve a blocked task until it clears.

## If something's off
- If the feed says "No work items," check that you haven't left a pocket or agent filter on in the left rail. Press `Esc` or use **clear** in the toolbar to reset all filters.
- The strip and feed share a time window. If recent work seems missing, switch the window to **All**.
- Refresh with the circular arrow button in the toolbar if a task's status looks stale.
