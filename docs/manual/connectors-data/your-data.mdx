# Your data: objects and links

**What it is.** A structured view of the records your assistant works with — customers, orders, products, invoices, bookings — shown as **Objects**, plus the relationships between them, shown as **Links**. Both live in a pocket's data panel, alongside **Capabilities**.

**When you'd use it.** When you want to browse the actual records behind your assistant's answers, see how they connect, and confirm the data is real and current.

## Browse your objects
1. Open a pocket and open its data panel.
2. Select the **Objects** tab.
3. Pick a type from the left (for example, **Customer** or **Order**). Each type shows a count.
4. Scan the table of records. Use the search box to find one by name.
5. Select a record to see its full properties and what it's linked to.

## Explore the links between records
1. In the same data panel, select the **Links** tab.
2. Pick a relationship (for example, **Customer → Order**) to see how records connect.
3. Each relationship shows a count and a few sample pairs so you can sanity-check the data.

## Where the data comes from
1. Objects fill in as your connected sources bring records in — see [Connect your tools](/manual/connectors-data/connect-your-tools).
2. The **Source** column on each record shows where it came from (for example, Stripe or a CSV).
3. When a source ingests records, the live data replaces the starter sample set.

## Tips
- Start with the **Objects** tab to confirm the records exist, then use **Links** to check the relationships are right.
- The **Source** column is the fastest way to tell a real, connected record from a placeholder.

## If something's off
- Before any source has brought in records, both tabs show a built-in sample set (a coffee-shop demo with customers, orders, and products). Those numbers are illustrative, not your data — connect a source to see real records take over.
- If the counts look suspiciously round or the names look like a demo (Acme Corp, Oat Milk Latte), you're still looking at the sample set. Confirm by checking the **Source** column.
- This view is for browsing, not editing. To change records, work through the connected source or ask your assistant.
