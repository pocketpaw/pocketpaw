# Your knowledge base

**What it is.** A single place that lists every article your workspace and its assistants have taken in — from files, web links, or pasted text. The page is called **Workspace Knowledge**.

**When you'd use it.** When you want to see what your assistants already know, find a specific source, or check what each assistant has learned.

## Browse what your workspace knows
1. Select **Knowledge** in the top navigation.
2. Scan the list. Each row shows the article title, where it came from, and when it was last updated.
3. A tag on each row shows the scope: **Workspace** for shared knowledge, or **Agent** for an item one assistant learned.

## Find a specific article
1. On the **Knowledge** page, type into the **Filter by source or title** box.
2. The list narrows as you type, matching against the title and the source.
3. To see only one assistant's knowledge, open the filter dropdown and pick that agent, or pick **Workspace scope only**.
4. Select **Refresh** to reload the list after new items are added.

## Add knowledge
1. There's no add button on the **Knowledge** page itself — it's a read-only overview.
2. To bring in new material, open any assistant's **Knowledge** tab and add a file, a URL, or raw text there.
3. New items appear on the **Workspace Knowledge** page after you select **Refresh**.

## Tips
- The footer tells you how many of the total articles you're seeing and how many assistants exist in the workspace.
- The source field is whatever the item came from — a web address, a filename, or a tag you set when you added it. Search against it to group related sources.

## If something's off
- An empty page with "No knowledge yet" means nothing has been added. Add a file, URL, or text from an assistant's **Knowledge** tab to populate it.
- The dropdown filter (by agent or workspace) reloads from the server, but the **Filter by source or title** box only narrows what's already loaded — clear it if results look thinner than expected.
